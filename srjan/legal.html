3. Copyright

3.1 Governing statute

Srjan operates under the Copyright Act, 1957. Under Section 22 of that Act, copyright in a published artistic work subsists until sixty years from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the author dies. When that term ends, the work is free for all persons to use.

3.2 Basis for display

Srjan displays a work only where one of the following applies: the term of copyright in the work has ended; the work is made available under a licence that permits its display; or the owner of the copyright has given permission.

A work whose term of copyright has not ended is withheld. It is released automatically on the first day after its term ends.

Srjan does not rely on the fair dealing exceptions in Section 52 of the Act to show a work to the public. Photographs of works of architecture are made and published under Section 52(1)(s), which permits the making or publishing of a photograph of a work of architecture.

3.3 Moral rights

The right of an author to claim authorship of a work, and to restrain its distortion, continues after the term of copyright ends (Section 57). Srjan names the creator of every work and shows each work in full in the viewer.

3.4 Use outside India

The term of copyright differs from country to country. Srjan applies the law of India. A visitor who copies or reuses a work shown on the Site is responsible for compliance with the law of the visitor's own country.

5. Accuracy of content

Srjan names the creator of each work and describes the work on the basis of standard published scholarship. Where an attribution is traditional, conventional or contested, the entry says so. Descriptive text is offered for general knowledge. Srjan corrects any error that is brought to its attention.

6. Notices, corrections and grievances

Any owner, creator or rights holder may notify Srjan that a work is shown without right, that an entry contains an error, or that content should be removed. A notice must state the name and contact details of the sender, identify the work and the page on which it appears, describe the right claimed, and state the action requested.

Notices and grievances are received through Srjan's Instagram page, @srjan.co. Srjan acknowledges each complaint within twenty four hours and resolves it within fifteen days, in accordance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. Where a notice shows that a work is displayed without right, Srjan suspends its display while the notice is examined.

Grievance Officer: The Grievance Officer, Srjan, through @srjan.co.

8. Privacy notice

8.1 Responsibility

Srjan is the Data Fiduciary for personal data collected through the Site, and it processes that data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

8.2 Data collected and purpose

Applications by creators. Srjan collects your name, email address, phone number (if you give one), city, country, biography, links, portrait and artwork files. Srjan uses them to review your application, to contact you, and, if your application is approved, to publish your exhibition. Your email address and phone number are never shown publicly. Your name, location, biography, portrait, statement and artworks are shown publicly once your exhibition is approved.

Enquiries. When a visitor sends an enquiry about a work, Srjan collects the visitor's name, email address and message, and passes them to the creator concerned or handles them on the creator's behalf.

Visits. The count of visitors is a single number and is not linked to any person. The figure for visitors viewing now uses a random temporary code that identifies no person and is discarded when the page is closed.

Saved works. The list of works you save is kept only in your own browser.

8.3 Consent

Srjan processes personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit a form. You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.

8.4 Sharing and transfer

Srjan does not sell personal data. Srjan shares it only with the creator concerned in the case of an enquiry, and with the service providers that host the Site and its database, who process it on Srjan's behalf. Those providers may store data on servers outside India. Any such transfer is made as the Act permits.

8.5 Retention

Srjan keeps personal data for as long as an exhibition is displayed and for as long as is needed to deal with related enquiries. Srjan then erases it, unless a law requires it to be kept. Srjan acts on a verified request for erasure within thirty days.

8.6 Your rights

You have the right to obtain a summary of the personal data Srjan holds about you, to have it corrected, completed or updated, to have it erased, to seek redress of a grievance, and to nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to do so. To exercise any of these rights, write to Srjan through @srjan.co.

8.7 Children

The Site is intended for persons who are eighteen years of age or older. Srjan does not knowingly collect the personal data of a child without the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian.

8.8 Security

Srjan applies reasonable security safeguards to the personal data it holds, as the Act requires, and it will give the notices that the Act requires in the event of a personal data breach.

8.9 Cookies and third parties

The Site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores your saved works and your display preferences in your browser. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server records. The Site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your device contacts Google when a page opens.

8.10 Complaints

Raise any complaint about the handling of your personal data with Srjan first, through @srjan.co. If the matter is not resolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Act.
