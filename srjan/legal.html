3. Copyright

3.1Governing statute. Srjan acts in accordance with the Copyright Act, 1957. Under Section 22 of the Act, copyright in a published artistic work subsists until sixty years from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the author dies. Upon the expiry of that term the work enters the public domain.

3.2Basis of display. Srjan displays a work from its catalogue only where one of the following conditions is satisfied: (a) the term of copyright in the work has expired; (b) the work is made available under a licence that permits its display; or (c) the owner of the copyright has given permission for its display.

3.3Works withheld. A work in which copyright still subsists is withheld from display and is released automatically upon the expiry of its term.

3.4Statutory exceptions. Srjan does not rely upon the exceptions provided in Section 52 of the Act for the display of any work to the public. Photographs of works of architecture are made and published in exercise of Section 52(1)(s) of the Act.

3.5Moral rights. The right of an author to claim authorship of a work, and to restrain its distortion, continues after the term of copyright has expired (Section 57 of the Act). Srjan names the creator of every work and presents each work in full in the viewer.

3.6Use outside India. The term of copyright differs from country to country. Srjan applies the law of India. A visitor who copies or reuses a work shown on the Site is solely responsible for compliance with the law of the visitor's own country.

5.1Attribution of works. Srjan attributes each work to its creator and describes it on the basis of standard published scholarship. Where an attribution is traditional, conventional or disputed, the relevant entry so states.

6. Notices, corrections and grievances

6.1Notice. Any owner, creator or rights holder may notify Srjan that a work is displayed without right, that an entry contains an error, or that content ought to be removed. A notice shall state the name and contact details of the sender, identify the work and the page on which it appears, describe the right claimed, and state the action requested.

6.2Manner of notice. Notices and grievances shall be sent through the Instagram page of Srjan, @srjan.co.

6.3Time limits. Srjan shall acknowledge each complaint within twenty four hours and shall resolve it within fifteen days, in accordance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

6.4Interim suspension. Where a notice shows that a work is displayed without right, Srjan shall suspend its display while the notice is examined.

6.5Grievance Officer. The Grievance Officer of Srjan may be contacted through @srjan.co.

The creator confirms that he or she is at least eighteen years of age; that he or she created each work, or has the right to show it, including every photograph and the work of every person credited; and that nothing submitted infringes the rights of any other person. 8. Privacy notice

8.1Responsibility. Srjan is the Data Fiduciary in respect of personal data collected through the Site, and it processes that data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

8.2Applications by creators. Srjan collects the name, email address, telephone number (where supplied), city, country, biography, links, portrait and artwork files of the applicant. These are used to review the application, to communicate with the applicant and, where the application is approved, to publish the exhibition. The email address and telephone number are never displayed publicly. The name, location, biography, portrait, statement and artworks are displayed publicly once the exhibition is approved.

8.3Enquiries. Where a visitor sends an enquiry about a work, Srjan collects the name, email address and message of the visitor and passes them to the creator concerned, or deals with them on the creator's behalf.

8.4Visits. The count of visitors is a single number and is not linked to any person. The figure for visitors viewing at present uses a random temporary code that identifies no person and is discarded when the page is closed.

8.5Saved works. The list of works saved by a visitor is kept only in the visitor's own browser.

8.6Consent. Srjan processes personal data on the basis of the consent that a person gives by submitting a form. Consent may be withdrawn at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.

8.7Disclosure and transfer. Srjan does not sell personal data. It discloses personal data only to the creator concerned, in the case of an enquiry, and to the service providers who host the Site and its database and who process the data on behalf of Srjan. Those providers may store data on servers outside India, and any such transfer is made as the Act permits.

8.8Retention. Srjan retains personal data for as long as an exhibition is displayed and for as long as is necessary to deal with related enquiries, and it then erases the data unless a law requires its retention. Srjan acts upon a verified request for erasure within thirty days.

8.9Rights of the data principal. Every person has the right to obtain a summary of the personal data that Srjan holds about that person; to have it corrected, completed or updated; to have it erased; to seek redress of a grievance; and to nominate another person to exercise these rights in the event of death or incapacity. These rights may be exercised by writing to Srjan through @srjan.co.

8.10Children. The Site is intended for persons who have attained the age of eighteen years. Srjan does not knowingly collect the personal data of a child without the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian.

8.11Security. Srjan applies reasonable security safeguards to the personal data it holds, as the Act requires, and it shall give the notices that the Act requires in the event of a personal data breach.

8.12Cookies and third parties. The Site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores the saved works and display preferences of a visitor in the visitor's browser. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which maintains its own server records, and it loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so that the visitor's device contacts Google when a page opens.

8.13Complaints. A complaint concerning the handling of personal data shall first be addressed to Srjan through @srjan.co. If the matter is not resolved, the complainant may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Act.

9.1Governing law. These terms, and the use of the Site, are governed by the laws of India. The courts of competent jurisdiction in India shall have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them.
