1. About Srjan

3. Copyright

3.1Term of copyright. Under Section 22 of the Copyright Act, 1957, copyright in a published artistic work lasts until sixty years from the beginning of the calendar year that follows the year in which the author dies. When that term ends, the work enters the public domain.

3.2When Srjan displays a work. Srjan displays a work from its catalogue only if (a) its term of copyright has ended, (b) it is made available under a licence that permits its display, or (c) the owner of the copyright has given permission. A work that is still in copyright is withheld and is released automatically on the day after its term ends.

3.3Statutory exceptions. Srjan does not rely on the exceptions in Section 52 of the Act to show any work to the public. Photographs of works of architecture are made and published under Section 52(1)(s), which permits the making and publishing of a photograph of a work of architecture.

3.4Moral rights. Under Section 57 of the Act, an author keeps the right to claim authorship of a work and to restrain its distortion after the term of copyright has ended. Srjan names the creator of every work and shows each work in full in the viewer.

3.5Other countries. The term of copyright differs between countries. Srjan applies the law of India. A visitor who copies or reuses a work outside the Site is responsible for complying with the law of the visitor's own country.

5. Accuracy of content

5.1Attribution and description. Srjan attributes each work to its creator and describes it on the basis of standard published scholarship. Where an attribution is traditional, conventional or disputed, the entry says so. The text is general information.

6. Notices, corrections and grievances

6.1Who may give notice. Any owner, creator or rights holder may give notice that a work is shown without right, and any person may give notice of an error or of content that should be removed. A notice must state the sender's name and contact details, identify the work and the page on which it appears, state the right claimed or the error found, and state the action requested.

6.2Contact channel. Notices, grievances and requests under Section 8 are sent through the Instagram page of Srjan, @srjan.co (the Contact Channel).

6.3Time limits. Srjan acknowledges each complaint within twenty four hours and resolves it within fifteen days, as the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 require.

6.4Interim suspension. Where a notice shows that a work is shown without right, Srjan suspends its display while the notice is examined.

6.5Grievance Officer. The Grievance Officer of Srjan receives complaints through the Contact Channel.

7. Artist terms

Version 4. These terms apply to every creator who hosts an exhibition or features work on Srjan.

7.1Ownership. The creator keeps the copyright in every work submitted, and the photographers, designers and makers whom the creator credits keep theirs. These terms transfer no ownership to Srjan.

7.2Licence to display. By submitting a work, the creator grants Srjan a licence, which is worldwide, free of charge and not exclusive, to display images of the work in low or medium resolution, with the creator's name and the details supplied, on the Site and in material that promotes the exhibition. The creator remains free to show the work elsewhere. Srjan will not sell the images, give them to any other person, or use them to train machine learning systems.

7.3Confirmations. The creator confirms that he or she is at least eighteen years old; that he or she created each work or has the right to show it, including every photograph and the work of every person credited; and that nothing submitted infringes the rights of any other person. The creator shall not submit content that is unlawful, hateful, sexual in any way involving minors, or that misleads a buyer about authorship or authenticity.

7.4Review. Srjan reviews every submission before publication. It may approve a submission, decline it, or ask for changes, and it need not give a reason. Approval is not an endorsement of any work.

7.5Withdrawal. The creator may ask at any time for an exhibition or a work to be removed, and Srjan removes it within seven days. Srjan may also remove content that breaches these terms or is the subject of a valid complaint.

7.6Sales. Where a creator marks a work as available, visitors may send an enquiry through Srjan. Any sale is a contract between the creator and the buyer. Srjan charges no fee on sales made during the launch period, unless it has agreed otherwise with the creator in writing. A fee introduced later is announced before it applies and does not apply to earlier sales.

7.7Protection of images. Srjan shows images at a reduced size and disables easy downloading. No website can prevent copying completely, so the creator should keep full size files private.

8. Privacy notice

8.1Responsibility. Srjan is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data collected through the Site and processes it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

8.2Applications by creators. Srjan collects the applicant's name, email address, telephone number (if given), city, country, biography, links, portrait and artwork files. It uses them to review the application, to contact the applicant and, if the application is approved, to publish the exhibition. The email address and telephone number are never shown publicly. The name, location, biography, portrait, statement and artworks are shown publicly once the exhibition is approved.

8.3Enquiries. When a visitor sends an enquiry about a work, Srjan collects the visitor's name, email address and message and passes them to the creator concerned, or deals with them on the creator's behalf.

8.4Visits and saved works. The count of visitors is one number and is not linked to any person. The figure for visitors viewing now uses a random temporary code that identifies no person and is discarded when the page closes. The works a visitor saves are kept only in the visitor's own browser.

8.5Consent. Srjan processes personal data on the basis of the consent that a person gives by submitting a form. A person may withdraw consent at any time, and withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.

8.6Disclosure and transfer. Srjan does not sell personal data. It discloses personal data only to the creator concerned, for an enquiry, and to the providers that process it for Srjan: GitHub, which hosts the Site, and Supabase, which hosts the database that stores applications and enquiries. Supabase may store data on servers outside India, and Srjan makes that transfer as the Act permits.

8.7Retention. Srjan keeps an application for as long as the exhibition is displayed and erases it within thirty days after the exhibition is removed. It erases a declined application within ninety days and an enquiry twelve months after receipt. It erases any personal data within thirty days of a verified request for erasure, unless a law requires that data to be kept.

8.8Rights. Every person may obtain a summary of the personal data Srjan holds about that person, have it corrected, completed or updated, have it erased, seek redress of a grievance, and nominate another person to exercise these rights in the event of death or incapacity. Srjan responds to a request made through the Contact Channel within thirty days.

8.9Age and children. The Site is directed to a general audience and not to children, and its forms are for persons aged eighteen or over. Eighteen is higher than the age at which a person may give consent under the laws of the principal jurisdictions: India eighteen, the European Union sixteen (lowered to thirteen by some member states), the United Kingdom thirteen, and the United States thirteen under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. Srjan does not knowingly collect the personal data of a person under eighteen. If it finds that it holds such data, it erases the data, unless a parent or lawful guardian has given verifiable consent. Srjan does not track or monitor the behaviour of visitors and shows no advertising, so it does no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children, which Section 9 of the Act prohibits.

8.10Security. Every page of the Site is served over an encrypted connection. Access to submitted data is limited to the administrator of Srjan. If a personal data breach occurs, Srjan notifies the Data Protection Board of India and each affected person, as the Act requires.

8.11Cookies and third parties. The Site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a visitor's saved works and display preferences in the visitor's browser. GitHub keeps its own server records of requests to the Site, and the Site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so the visitor's device contacts Google when a page opens.

8.12Visitors outside India. The Site is operated from India. Where the data protection law of another country applies to Srjan's processing of the personal data of a person in that country, Srjan complies with that law to the extent that it applies. Srjan complies with every order or direction that a competent court or authority makes under a law that applies to Srjan.

8.13Complaints. A person who has a complaint about the handling of personal data writes first to Srjan through the Contact Channel. If the matter is not resolved, the person may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Act.
