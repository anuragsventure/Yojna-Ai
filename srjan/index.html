Artist rights and terms

Version 1. These terms apply to creators who host an exhibition or feature work on Srjan.

1. You keep your rights

You keep the copyright in every work you submit, and the photographers, designers and makers you credit keep theirs. Nothing in these terms transfers ownership to Srjan.

2. Licence to display

By submitting a work you give Srjan a licence to display images of it in low or medium resolution, with your name and the details you supply, on this website and in material that promotes your exhibition. The licence is not exclusive, so you remain free to show your work anywhere else. Srjan will not sell your images, pass them to others, or use them to train machine learning systems.

3. Your responsibilities

You confirm that you created the works or have the right to show them, including any photographs and the work of anyone you credit, and that they do not infringe the rights of anyone else. Do not submit content that is unlawful, hateful, sexual in any way involving minors, or that misleads buyers about authorship or authenticity.

4. Review and approval

Every submission is reviewed by Srjan before publication. We may approve, decline or ask for changes, and we do not have to give a reason. Approval is not an endorsement of any work.

5. Removal

You may ask us to remove your exhibition or any work at any time by writing to the contact address on this site. We will remove it promptly. We may also remove content that breaks these terms or that we receive a valid complaint about.

6. Sales and enquiries

When you mark a work as available, visitors can send an enquiry through Srjan. Any sale is a contract between you and the buyer. Unless agreed with you in writing, Srjan does not take a fee on sales made during the launch period. Any future fees will be announced in advance and will never apply retroactively.

7. Protecting your images

We show images at a reduced size and turn off easy downloading, but no website can fully prevent copying. Please keep your full size files to yourself. Visitors may not copy, reuse or redistribute works shown here.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. We will tell active artists by email before significant changes take effect.

Privacy

We collect the details you enter in the application form: your name, email, phone number (if given), city and country, biography, links, portrait and artwork files. Your email address and phone number are used only to contact you about your application and any enquiries, and are never shown publicly. Your name, location, biography, portrait, statement and artworks are shown publicly if your exhibition is approved.

Enquiries from visitors (name, email and message) are shared with the relevant artist or handled by Srjan on their behalf. We count visits to the site in aggregate and do not use advertising trackers. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to the contact address on this site.
