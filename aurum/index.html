Spotlight on India

Bharat Gallery

From the lamp-lit murals of Ajanta to the miniature courts of the Mughals and Rajputs, India has one of the oldest and richest painting traditions in the world.

Raja Ravi Varma (1848-1906), a prince of Kilimanoor in Kerala, taught himself European oil painting and used it to retell the Mahabharata, the Ramayana and the plays of Kalidasa. When he set up his own press and printed his gods and heroines as affordable oleographs, millions of Indians saw those stories for the first time.

Then came the Bengal School of Abanindranath Tagore and Nandalal Bose, and the village women of Amrita Sher-Gil. Together they show how India found its own modern eye.

Artist rights and terms

Version 1. These terms apply to artists who host an exhibition on AURUM.

1. You own your work

You keep the copyright in every work you submit. Nothing in these terms transfers ownership to AURUM.

2. Licence to display

By submitting a work you give AURUM a non-exclusive, worldwide licence to display low and medium resolution images of it, with your name and the details you supply, on this website and in material that promotes your exhibition. AURUM will not sell, license to third parties or use your images to train machine learning systems.

3. Your responsibilities

You confirm that you created the works, that you have the right to show them, and that they do not infringe the rights of anyone else. Do not submit content that is unlawful, hateful, sexually explicit involving minors, or that misleads buyers about authorship or authenticity.

4. Review and approval

Every submission is reviewed by AURUM before publication. We may approve, decline or ask for changes, and we do not have to give a reason. Approval is not an endorsement of any work.

5. Removal

You may ask us to remove your exhibition or any work at any time by writing to the contact address on this site. We will remove it promptly. We may also remove content that breaks these terms or that we receive a valid complaint about.

6. Sales and enquiries

When you mark a work as available, visitors can send an enquiry through AURUM. Any sale is a contract between you and the buyer. Unless agreed with you in writing, AURUM does not take a fee on sales made during the launch period. Any future fees will be announced in advance and will never apply retroactively.

7. Protecting your images

We show reduced-size images and disable casual downloading, but no website can fully prevent copying. Please keep full-resolution files to yourself. Visitors may not copy, reuse or redistribute works shown here.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. We will tell active artists by email before significant changes take effect.

Privacy

We collect the details you enter in the application form: your name, email, phone number (if given), city and country, biography, links, portrait and artwork files. Your email address and phone number are used only to contact you about your application and any enquiries, and are never shown publicly. Your name, location, biography, portrait, statement and artworks are shown publicly if your exhibition is approved.

Enquiries from visitors (name, email and message) are shared with the relevant artist or handled by AURUM on their behalf. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to the contact address on this site.
